Welcome to the Lab

The Wilson-Medimorec MIND Research Lab is a collaborative research group co-led by Dr Christopher Wilson and Dr Srdan Medimorec, who are based in the Department of Psychology at Teesside University. The lab’s work is organised into two primary research streams, each led by a Principal Investigator.

The Cognitive Neuroscience of Risk & Gambling Harms.

Learn more about gambling research here

Language, meta-cognition, implicit learning.

Learn more about language and learning research here

We use a range of methods, including functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS), eye-tracking, and virtual reality, to study these processes in both healthy and clinical populations.

NoneJoining the lab

If you are interested in joining the lab, the fist step is reading the Lab Manual. This will give you an overview of the lab’s research, methods, and culture.
